One thing left to set up

Drafting pitches and building sample sites needs an OpenRouter key — that's the service that runs the AI. It's free to create, and you pay only for what you use: about 4¢ per business you pitch in full.

  1. Go to openrouter.ai/keys and sign in
  2. Click Create key, then copy it (you only see it once)
  3. Paste it below

Everything else works without this — searching, the map, your notes. Only the two AI buttons need it.
